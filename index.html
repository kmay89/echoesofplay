Echoes of Play
What this is
A quiet, offline playground for reflection. A card game for your thoughts, a breathing screen, postcards for memories, and a vault to keep them in. One HTML file. Free, open source, built by ERRERlabs.
You can tell the room how you're arriving and it retunes its colors and pace. The optional music (♪) is a handful of soft notes generated on your device as you listen — there are no recordings, no files, and nothing streamed.
Why it's made
Slowing down with a thought, breathing on purpose, and telling the story of a memory are old, ordinary, human practices. Most digital versions of them come with an account, a subscription, and a data pipeline. This one comes with none of those. That's the whole idea.
What we think it's good for
A few quiet minutes. A second angle on a stuck thought. A slower breath. A memory written down in someone's own words while they can still tell it.
And what it is not: not therapy, not counseling, not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not AI. Every word here was written by hand; nothing reads or analyzes what you write. We claim no health benefits. If reflective writing stirs up more than you want to carry alone, stop, and consider talking to someone you trust or a professional.

Your privacy
Nothing you write ever leaves this device. No server, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no AI, no network connections. Your reflections live in this browser's local storage, controlled entirely by you.
Verify it: open your browser's developer tools and watch the network tab. It stays empty.
One honest caveat: local storage belongs to your browser. Clearing browser data will erase your entries. If something matters, export it below.
Full policies: Privacy · Terms · Cookies
A note on breathing
The breathing screen is an invitation, not an instruction — you set the pace and you can stop anytime. Slow or held breathing can make some people lightheaded. If that happens, just breathe normally. Don't use paced breathing while driving or anywhere you need your balance.
Your data
Export creates a file on your device — nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload to. Delete removes every entry from this browser, permanently. We can't recover it, because we never had it.
Keep it close
This room lives happily on your Home Screen — it opens instantly, full screen, and works with no connection at all.
  • iPhone & iPad — open this page in Safari, tap the Share square, then “Add to Home Screen”
  • Android — tap the browser’s ⋮ menu, then “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”)
When a new version ships, the app quietly notices and offers a one-tap refresh. Your reflections live in this browser’s storage, not in the app’s files — updates never touch them.
Pass it on
The best way to support this is to hand it to someone who needs a quiet room.
Sharing uses your device's own share sheet or clipboard. The app itself still sends nothing, to anyone, ever.

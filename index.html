Three commitments

Radically honest. Not AI. Hand-written prompts. It cannot understand you. It offers angles. Your brain does the rest.

Radically free. No cost. No ads. No subscription. No paywall. Open source. Apache 2.0. Forever.

Radically private. Zero network connections. No server. No API. No analytics. No cookies. These are your thoughts and memories. We cherish them. They never leave your device.

What this cannot do

Diagnose. Prescribe. Detect crisis. Replace therapy, a friend, or a crisis line. Understand you.

Memory Garden

For sitting with people you love — including those whose memories are changing. Gentle prompts. Every word saved instantly. Export anytime. Pass it on.

Audio recording is not included. Current browser speech APIs send audio to external servers, which would break the privacy commitment. When local-only speech recognition becomes reliable, it will be added. No shortcuts.
